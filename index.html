Warisan & Jiwa Rumah Heritage & Soul of The House

Arsitektur yang Bernapas,
Keheningan yang Menyembuhkan Architecture That Breathes,
Silence That Restores

Villa Joglo dibangun bukan hanya sebagai tempat peristirahatan, tetapi sebagai karya seni hidup. Kami merestorasi struktur Rumah Joglo antik berusia lebih dari satu abad yang dipahat tangan oleh empu kayu Jawa tempo dulu. Menggunakan pasak kayu tradisional tanpa paku besi, struktur ini berdiri fleksibel, kokoh, dan selaras dengan getaran bumi. Villa Joglo is conceived not merely as an accommodation, but as a living piece of cultural artistry. We meticulously restored centuries-old antique Joglo pavilions hand-carved by master artisans. Assembled using traditional wooden mortise-and-tenon joints without a single iron nail, each structure breathes harmoniously with the earth.

Di bawah struktur atap bertingkat Tumpangsari dan empat tiang utama Soko Guru, ruang bernapas secara leluasa. Angin lembah mengalir menyejukkan ruangan secara alami tanpa sekat yang mengurung, membiarkan aroma kayu jati tua berpadu dengan wanginya rerumputan basah seusai hujan. Beneath the cascading Tumpangsari ceiling and the monumental Soko Guru pillars, spatial freedom reigns. Mountain breezes circulate uninhibitedly across open pavilions, marrying the grounding scent of ancient teak with the fresh fragrance of rain-soaked flora.

Informasi & Pertanyaan Tamu Guest Information & FAQ

Pertanyaan yang Sering Diajukan Frequently Asked Questions

Segala informasi penting seputar kenyamanan, fasilitas privat, dan kemudahan reservasi di Villa Joglo. Essential details regarding your stay, private amenities, and effortless reservation at Villa Joglo.

Berapa tarif menginap per malam di Villa Joglo dan apa saja yang sudah termasuk? What are the nightly rates at Villa Joglo and what is included?
Tarif menginap berkisar antara Rp 2.950.000 (Joglo Sekar) hingga Rp 5.200.000 (Joglo Lawas 2-Bedroom) per malam. Seluruh tarif sudah termasuk santapan artisanal organic breakfast untuk 2-6 orang, complimentary welcome herbal jamu drink, afternoon tea di teras privat, akses Wi-Fi berkecepatan tinggi, serta layanan personal butler 24 jam. Nightly rates range from Rp 2,950,000 (Joglo Sekar) to Rp 5,200,000 (Joglo Lawas 2-Bedroom). All stays include bespoke organic breakfast for 2-6 guests, complimentary welcoming herbal jamu, afternoon tea on your private terrace, high-speed Wi-Fi, and 24-hour dedicated butler service.
Apakah setiap unit villa dilengkapi dengan kolam renang privat (private pool)? Does each villa unit feature a private pool?
Ya, sebagian besar unit kami dirancang dengan fasilitas air privat eksklusif. Joglo Ageng memiliki private infinity pool sepanjang 10 meter menghadap hutan tropis, Joglo Tirta memiliki private plunge pool di atas kolam teratai, Joglo Lawas dilengkapi inner courtyard pool privat, dan Joglo Sekar memiliki bathtub batu sungai alam semi-terbuka. Yes, our pavilions are crafted with exclusive private aquatic amenities. Joglo Ageng boasts a 10-meter private infinity pool facing the tropical forest, Joglo Tirta features an intimate private plunge pool over the lotus lagoon, Joglo Lawas offers a private inner courtyard dip pool, and Joglo Sekar features a soothing semi-outdoor river stone bathtub.
Bagaimana cara reservasi langsung (direct booking) untuk jaminan harga terbaik? How do I make a direct reservation for the best guaranteed rate?
Anda dapat langsung menghubungi concierge kami melalui tombol WhatsApp Direct Concierge resmi di website ini. Melalui direct booking, Anda dijamin mendapatkan harga terendah tanpa komisi perantara (OTAs), jaminan unit favorit, serta keistimewaan early check-in & late check-out fleksibel. You can directly connect with our dedicated concierge via the official WhatsApp Direct Concierge buttons on this site. Direct booking guarantees our lowest rates with zero third-party commission fees, preferred suite selection, and flexible early check-in or late check-out privileges.
Berapa jarak dan waktu tempuh Villa Joglo dari Bandara Yogyakarta (YIA) dan pusat kota? What is the distance and travel time from Yogyakarta Airport (YIA) and the city?
Villa Joglo berjarak sekitar 45 menit perjalanan dari Bandara Internasional Yogyakarta (YIA) dan 35 menit dari pusat budaya Yogyakarta (Malioboro & Keraton). Kami menyediakan layanan penjemputan bandara privat ber-AC dengan pengemudi ramah atas permintaan tamu. Villa Joglo is situated approximately 45 minutes from Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) and 35 minutes from historic downtown Yogyakarta (Malioboro & Keraton). We provide private chauffeured air-conditioned airport transfers upon request.
Berapa kapasitas maksimal tamu untuk setiap tipe unit Joglo? What is the maximum guest occupancy for each pavilion?
Joglo Tirta dan Joglo Sekar dirancang untuk 2 orang dewasa (sangat ideal untuk honeymoon). Joglo Ageng dapat menampung hingga 4 tamu dengan opsi extra bed. Sedangkan Joglo Lawas memiliki 2 kamar tidur utama mandiri yang dapat menampung 4 hingga 6 orang keluarga dengan leluasa. Joglo Tirta and Joglo Sekar are tailored for 2 adults (ideal for couples and honeymoons). Joglo Ageng accommodates up to 4 guests with luxury extra bedding. Joglo Lawas offers two separate master suites, comfortably accommodating 4 to 6 family members.
